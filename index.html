I know trying to design and manufacture a personal spaceship all on my own might seem outlandish. But despite the doomer narrative around AI, I am hopeful for the future, and all I can guarantee from the present moment is that we will all be awed and surprised by AI capability, but also by human flourishing.

The future I hope for is one where anybody, in any economic situation, can explore existence the way they wish. I hope to be continually surprised and delighted by the thriving of my peers, fellow eternal students.
